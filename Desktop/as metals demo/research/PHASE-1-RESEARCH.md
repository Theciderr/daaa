# Phase 1: comparable website research

20 November 2026 · Eight businesses: four Indian, four international. Findings are based on publicly readable pages and linked galleries/contact routes; visual/browser performance and successful form submissions were not tested. Strengths and weaknesses below are design judgments, not conversion-rate claims. All proposals await approval.

| Reference | What works | Weakness / lesson for A.S |
|---|---|---|
| [Jaali.in, India](https://jaali.in/ornamental-pattern) | Named design codes, pattern families, material guidance and an explicit distinction between sample renderings and products. | Dense nested categories and image-only pattern links can separate selection from enquiry. Keep each A.S code beside its image and quote action. |
| [KK CNC Wood Designs, India](https://kkcncwooddesigns.com/) | Searchable/category-led catalogue; design names; quote near the opening; WhatsApp plus a short enquiry form with reference upload; local directions. | Long horizontal collections and broad craft claims require effort to evaluate. Use a small curated opening selection and accessible gallery filters; demonstrate detail through labelled images. |
| [Delta Designs, India](https://www.deltadesigns.in/) | Location and materials near the top; category browsing; a direct instruction to send a design code on WhatsApp. | “Where design meets precision” and lengthy company prose are interchangeable with competitors. Lead with actual products and Ambala rather than abstract quality claims. |
| [Bajrang Bali Plywood, India](https://bajrangbaliplywood.co.in/cnc-designs) | Search/use/style filters; size inputs; design preview and WhatsApp handoff connect browsing with a specific enquiry. | Technical router/file controls add substantial complexity for a homeowner. Offer a simple reference + approximate size route, with material optional. |
| [Miles & Lincoln, UK](https://www.milesandlincoln.com/projects) | Separates patterns from installations; project captions connect location, application and design, making the work easier to understand. | Huge project list and generic contact route make shortlisting harder. Use a curated gallery and carry the selected code into the quote. Do not borrow its client names as A.S proof. |
| [Parasoleil, USA](https://www.parasoleil.com/) · [Pattern library](https://www.parasoleil.com/patterns) | Clear patterns/products/work/resources structure; filterable named library; prominent Start Your Project; technical FAQs address specification uncertainty. | A multi-tool specification journey is more than a local mobile enquiry needs. Borrow material clarity, not its engineering claims or complicated architecture. |
| [Screen With Envy, UK](https://www.screenwithenvy.co.uk/) | Shopping paths by pattern, style and size; contextual product photography; quick product inspection and visible proof claims. | Repeated sale banners and competing shopping actions dilute calm browsing. A.S needs one quote action and no invented price, review or delivery promises. |
| [Lump Sculpture Studio, Australia](https://www.lump.com.au/) | Residential/commercial paths, named projects, material/finish information, workshop identity, contact prompts and attributed reviews give context to fabrication. | Large service navigation and a generic contact form ask visitors to organise the enquiry themselves. A.S can prefill product interest and code. Its separate contact route currently redirects to a contact-test URL, so preserve a straightforward direct fallback. |

## What we should do differently

**Let the catalogue answer “Can you make this?”** Present the four verified offers through a few large reference images, with a clear distinction between 2D cut-through jali and 3D carved relief. Retain catalogue names/codes and label illustrative applications. Avoid a carousel-only discovery path and vague premium/precision slogans.

**Make the next action concrete.** Use the brief's Get a Quote on WhatsApp above the fold, after gallery sections and in the footer, plus its mobile WhatsApp Quote/Call bar. Proposed handoff carries the selected AMF code, requirement and approximate size, with “not sure” for material. Plain WhatsApp remains available. The upload requirement needs an honest attachment method; do not imply files accompany a text link automatically.

**Earn trust with verifiable detail.** Use Akash Sharma's catalogue identity, local address, actual board/finish options and detail images. Material suitability, depth and finish are confirmed per project. Opening date comes from the brief. No fake installations, reviews, statistics or ordering promises. The catalogue alone does not confirm installation, shipping, turnaround or warranties.

**Recommended research direction for approval:** a restrained catalogue-led dark/gold site, image/code-to-WhatsApp continuity, and a short homeowner-friendly enquiry. Sitemap, final tokens, typography and layouts belong to Phase 2 and are not approved yet.
