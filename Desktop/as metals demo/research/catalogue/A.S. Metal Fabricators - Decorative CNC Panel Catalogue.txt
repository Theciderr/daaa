CUTTING & MATERIAL ENQUIRIES
Order a cut designer panel using a board we supply, or bring
your own suitable material.
We can cut most suitable sheet materials. Send the material
type, grade, thickness and dimensions so we can confirm
feasibility.
For ACP, aluminium and HPL Compact, customer-supplied
sheets are preferred. We can look into sourcing them on
request.
For damp or sheltered outdoor settings, ask about a
water-resistant grade and suitable edge treatment.

Laminated boards

edge banding or painted cut edges.
