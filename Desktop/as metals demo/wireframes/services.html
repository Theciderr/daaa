A few details before you enquire.

Which materials can I ask about?

MDF, HDF and HDHMR options depend on your design. For ACP, aluminium and HPL Compact, customer-supplied sheets are preferred; sourcing can be considered on request.

Can I bring my own board or design?

Yes. Share the reference and the material type, grade, thickness and dimensions so suitability can be reviewed.

What finish can I ask for?

Raw or primed board, Duco painting by arrangement, and suitable laminated boards for 2D work. Relief colour matching can be reviewed from a reference.
